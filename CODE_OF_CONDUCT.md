# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Valtors a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accountability, and prioritizing the community's interests.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, harassment, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to an organization owner through an established contact channel. Reports will be handled proportionately. Maintainers responsible for enforcement must respect reporters' privacy and recuse themselves when conflicted.

## Scope

This Code applies in Valtors project spaces and when representing Valtors publicly.
